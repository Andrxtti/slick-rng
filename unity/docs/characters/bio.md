## Brief

**Bio "The Ghost"** · 14 bracelets, the Main Event's final boss, "never explains" · ageless early-30s · 5'9", slight, sits
very still with his forearms on the rail and his chips stacked in perfect towers
- Face: mostly unseen. A fabric face mask with a loud, busy illustrated print (his only colour) covers nose to chin; above
  it, calm narrow eyes that move before his head does. Hair: thick straight brown bowl cut, fringe to the brows.
- Wardrobe: Standard — oversized white hoodie with small black lettering on the sleeve, the mask. Signature — the same in
  black. Iconic (the Main Event final) — the white hoodie under a black tux jacket, a new mask printed with 14 gold bracelets.
- Movement: almost none. Glances sideways at you without turning. Stacks and restacks. When he wins a pot he doesn't react;
  the camera does. Few lines; the mask means no lip-sync is needed for most of them (eyes and brows carry it).
- Replaces the old data look (buzz cut, goatee, tux, aviators).
