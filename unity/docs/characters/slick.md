# Slick — character brief and Daz dial sheet

Source: the Confirmed Fire logo (docs/icons/slick-badge.jpg, slick-dwi.jpg): a young man bent over his chips, dark
tousled hair, zip jacket, the 8-bit "deal with it" sunglasses. Slick is that figure made human and aged into the
stranger at the diner.

Steven, Oct 28: Slick is East Asian, in the cool, sharp-dressed street-racing-villain mould (his comparison: Johnny Tran
in The Fast and the Furious). He is modelled on poker player Eric Chang, "SlickRic". Type and attitude only: no
likeness of Eric or of any actor.

## Brief

**Slick** · the stranger from the diner, later the mentor · 34 · lean, 5'11", a player's stillness
- Face: East Asian. Lean and sharp: defined jaw, high cheekbones, straight nose, a mouth that rests almost smiling on
  one side. Light-to-medium warm skin, a little tired under the eyes (he lives at 2 AM). Dark brown almond eyes, calm
  heavy lids; the look is cool, never loud. Hair: black, thick, styled up and back with some height at the front, sides
  short and neat (not shaved). Clean-shaven or the lightest stubble; at most a thin, sharp line on the chin.
- The glasses are the signature: thin black rectangular frames, lenses dark enough to hide the eyes in daylight.
  Indoors at night he wears them pushed up or hooked on the collar; at the table they go on. The 8-bit version appears
  only in UI and the logo, never on the 3D character.
- Wardrobe: Standard — black zip jacket (mandarin collar, matte), charcoal tee, dark jeans, white trainers. Signature —
  the same silhouette in a black blazer over the tee, a steel watch. Iconic — black suit, no tie, the glasses, a single
  gold chip he turns over in his fingers.
- Movement: economy. Sits forward, forearms on the table, hands quiet except for the chip. Talks with his eyebrows.
  Never raises his voice; the push-in is the camera doing his emphasis.

## Daz Studio dial sheet (Genesis 9 Starter Essentials, free content only)

Oct 28 note: the free Genesis 9 content has no East Asian male face, no men's hair but an undercut, and no
stubble. Slick's real build is the MetaHuman route (Unreal); the Daz build below proved the pipeline only.

Load: Genesis 9 (Masculine base). Set everything from zero.

Body (Shaping tab → Actor)
- Masculine 100 · Body Size −15 · Body Height +5 · Muscularity −10 (lean, not thin)
- Shoulders Width +5 · Neck Length +5 · Hands Size 0

Head (Shaping → Head / Face; approximate, judge by eye against the logo)
- Head Oval +40 · Face Length +20 · Cheekbones Height +30 · Cheeks Hollow +25
- Jaw Width −10 · Chin Width −10 · Chin Depth +10
- Brow Height −10 (lower, heavier brow) · Brow Thickness +20
- Eyes Size −5 · Eyes Height −5 · Eyelids Upper Heavy +30 · Eye Bags +20 (the 2 AM look)
- Nose Bridge Height +10 · Nose Width −5 · Nose Length +5
- Mouth Width +5 · Lips Thickness −10 · Lip Corners Up (left side) +15
- Ears Size 0

Skin and eyes
- Genesis 9 base skin, warm-olive preset (or the base skin with Translucency Weight 0.5, warm tint). Eyes: dark brown.

Hair
- Starter Essentials short/medium male hair, black, pushed back; if only short is available, pick the longest and
  we add volume in Unity. Stubble: the base facial hair at low density, or a stubble texture (free).

Clothes (Standard look)
- Starter Essentials base outfit; recolour the top to matte black, trousers to dark denim. The jacket's zip and
  mandarin collar come from a free Daz freebie or Blender later; for the proof shot the base top in black is enough.

Glasses
- Any thin rectangular frames from the free content; lenses black at 90 % opacity. If none, skip: Unity gets a
  modelled pair (I make it).

Export (when it looks right)
- File → Send to → Daz to Unity (the bridge). Export: character with morphs (face morphs needed for lip-sync), subdivision 1,
  textures 2K. Target folder: C:\Dev\Confirmed Fire Dream\Assets\Daz\Slick. Then tell me; the Unity side is mine.
